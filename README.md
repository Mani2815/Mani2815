## About

I'm an MCA student who spent the last few months as an AI/ML Engineer Intern at JobJockey, building and shipping three production applications: an LLM-powered hiring assistant, a real-time voice AI agent, and an internal management portal. Outside of that, I've built a full-stack complaint-management platform, an ML fraud detection system, and a crime-prediction model trained on 638K+ records.

I work mainly in Python and JavaScript/TypeScript — React/Next.js on the frontend, FastAPI/Flask on the backend, and Postgres/Supabase for storage. Most of what I build sits at the intersection of a model and the product around it: getting an ML or LLM component working is one part of the job, wiring it into something reliable is the other.

## Projects

**JobJockey** — *Featured*
AI hiring platform with an LLM-powered assistant for resume screening and JD matching, plus a real-time voice AI agent for candidate interviews.
`Next.js` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `Supabase`
[Live](https://jobjockey.in)

**Civitas**
Full-stack complaint-management platform with role-based dashboards, an AI chat assistant, and an hourly priority-scoring engine.
`React` `Node.js` `MongoDB`
[Repo](https://github.com/Mani2815/civitas)

**Fraud Shield**
Fraud and phishing detection combining rule-based matching, an ML/NLP classifier, and OCR, with an explainable-AI layer for flagged results.
`Python` `Scikit-learn` `NLP`
[Repo](https://github.com/Mani2815/fraud-shield)

**Perpetrator ID Prediction**
Classification model trained on 638K+ homicide records to predict perpetrator age, gender, and victim relationship, deployed behind a Flask REST API.
`Python` `Flask` `XGBoost`
[Repo](https://github.com/Mani2815/crime-data-analysis)
